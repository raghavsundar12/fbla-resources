General Tips: Presentation & Conference Etiquette

  • Dress professionally – business attire shows confidence and respect.
  • Arrive early to your event room and introduce yourself politely to judges.
  • Speak clearly, make eye contact, and smile – confidence is key!
  • Practice your timing – most events have strict time limits.
  • Be respectful and supportive of other competitors.
  • At conferences, represent Wheeler FBLA proudly – act professionally at all times.
  • Stay organized: bring required materials like note cards or presentation slides.
  • Most importantly, have fun and network with other future business leaders!
